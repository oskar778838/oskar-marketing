# Lane 5 — Competitor Positioning

**Scope:** Where does Oskar's "indigo + ehrlich + Build-in-Public" brand sit on the German-language
affiliate / online-marketing-coach map of 2026? Is the position DISTINCT and CREDIBLE, or does it
vanish into the gold-hustle cluster — especially given the Pro page is itself a Mark Janzen
affiliate funnel?

**Method note (HONESTY MODE):** Competitor *visual palettes* and *prices* below come from public
landing pages fetched live via WebFetch, plus WebSearch result snippets. Where a competitor hides
price behind an application gate, I say so — I did **not** invent a number. I could **not** open
TikTok/Instagram profiles for any competitor (those platforms block automated fetch); follower
counts and engagement are therefore **"needs human verification"** throughout. The one local claim
in the brief — the Pro-page affiliate funnel — I verified by reading the file directly.

---

## 1. Local fact-check first (the load-bearing claim)

Verified in `pro/index.html`:

- Page `<title>` is literally `Mark Janzen Pro Mentoring` (`pro/index.html:12`). The H1 renders
  "Mark Janzen / Pro Mentoring" (`:92-93`). So the product being sold on Oskar's own domain is
  branded **Mark Janzen**, not Oskar.
- The 997 € CTA links to `https://www.digistore24.com/product/583561?aff=Bestproducts99978`
  (`pro/index.html:204`) — an **affiliate** link (`aff=` param), correctly carrying
  `rel="noopener noreferrer sponsored"` (`:209`) and a visible disclosure: "Ich erhalte eine
  Provision wenn du startest." (`:224-228`). Editorial/affiliate-disclosure rules: **passed.**
- So the brief's premise is **confirmed**: the headline "Pro" offer is a *resold third-party
  course*, and Oskar earns commission. Oskar's own paid service is the free `cal.eu` 30-min
  "Pro-Beratung" call (`:341-343`) that gates entry to that funnel.

This matters for the risk question because it changes what "credible and distinct" has to mean:
Oskar is not differentiating his *own* coaching product against the cluster — he is an **affiliate
of the cluster** wearing an indigo skin. The differentiation must therefore live entirely in the
**presentation layer** (brand, honesty, Build-in-Public), because the underlying product IS a
gold-cluster product.

## 3. Where Oskar sits — the two-axis read

Axis 1: **Visual palette** — gold ⇄ not-gold. Axis 2: **Tonality** — loud-hype ⇄ calm-honest.

- The cluster (Janzen, Przybylski, Shiripour, Slusarek) is overwhelmingly **gold/black + loud or
  scarcity-driven.** Gadzhi has migrated to **dark-minimal**, so "premium dark + restrained" is no
  longer uniquely Oskar's — the leader of the archetype is already there.
- Oskar's tokens are genuinely off-axis on *colour*: background `#F5F7F8` (Off-White Snow), accent
  `#5B5BD6` indigo, periwinkle `#A8A8FF`, ink `#0E1116`, Cormorant Garamond display + JetBrains
  Mono (per `CLAUDE.md` brand block; tokens in `src/styles/tokens.css`). **No gold, no black-luxe,
  no Lambo.** On a thumbnail grid next to the five competitors, the **light + indigo + editorial**
  look genuinely reads as a different species. That is a real, defensible visual distinction.
- On *tonality*, the site *claims* the calm-honest corner — meta description "Affiliate-Marketing
  das nicht aussieht wie Affiliate-Marketing." (`index.html:12`), section heading "System statt
  Hype" (`index.html:353`), and a real Build-in-Public artifact: the "Tag N" counter
  (`getBuildDay()` in `src/config.ts:33`; today = **Tag 32**, anchored 2026-04-29). The transparency
  device (public day-count, "echte Zahlen") is something **none** of the five competitors do — it
  is the single most distinct asset Oskar has.

**Verdict on DISTINCT:** Yes — *visually and in the transparency mechanic*, the brand is distinct.
Light-indigo-editorial-with-a-public-build-log is not occupied by any of the five.

## 4. Where it VANISHES into the cluster (the credibility risk)

The honest position is undermined by three self-inflicted contradictions:

1. **The brand literally names the cluster as its origin AND its destination is unfinished.**
   `index.html:22`: "Vom Gold-Cluster ins Indigo-Future." Stating you came *from* the gold-cluster
   only works if the product no longer behaves like it. But the product (§1) **is** a gold-cluster
   Digistore course (Mark Janzen, 997 €). So the pivot is cosmetic at the offer layer.

2. **"System statt Hype" — then the page ends on "Imperium."** Both the homepage
   (`index.html:963`) and the Pro page (`pro/index.html:373`) close their manifesto with the word
   **"Imperium."** ("Tag für Tag bauen. Stein für Stein. Imperium." — `pro/index.html:371-373`).
   "Imperium" is the canonical Tate/Gadzhi gold-hustle word. Anti-hype copy that climaxes on
   "Imperium" reads, to anyone fluent in this niche, as the cluster in a nicer font. This is the
   clearest single place the indigo/honest signal **collapses back into** the gold cluster.

3. **The funnel uses the exact mechanics the Verbraucherzentrale flags.** The Pro page runs hard
   binary qualification — "Pro ist NICHT für dich wenn… Du brauchst Garantie … geh in einen
   Festanstellungs-Job." (`pro/index.html:303-307`) — and "997 € sind kein Kleingeld."
   (`pro/index.html:276`). This is textbook high-ticket-coaching framing. It is *more honest* than
   most (it explicitly de-sells "Schnelle-Sieg" / "5-stelliger Monat in 30 Tagen",
   `pro/index.html:288-291`), which helps — but the architecture is the cluster's architecture.

**Net:** the *brand layer* escapes the cluster; the *offer + tonality climax* drag it back in. A
skeptical German buyer who reaches the Pro page sees: indigo wrapper → another guy's 997 € Digistore
course → "Imperium." The honest promise and the funnel mechanics are in tension.

---

## 5. Concrete, evidenced fixes (no platitudes)

1. **Kill "Imperium."** Replace the manifesto climax on both `index.html:963` and
   `pro/index.html:373`. One word does measurable brand damage by re-coding the whole page as
   hustle. Swap for something matching "System statt Hype" — e.g. a build/craft word, not an
   empire word. (Lowest-effort, highest-signal fix in this lane.)

2. **Make the affiliate relationship a *trust* asset, not a footnote.** The disclosure at
   `pro/index.html:224-228` is compliant but defensive. Reframe at the top of the Pro page:
   state plainly that Pro is Mark Janzen's program, that Oskar is using/recommending it, and *why*.
   In a category the Verbraucherzentrale has poisoned, "I'm an affiliate and here's exactly how I
   get paid" is the most on-brand ("ehrlich") move available — and it converts the §4-risk #1 into
   proof of the honesty positioning.

3. **Lean harder on the one thing no competitor has: the public build-log.** None of Janzen,
   Przybylski, Shiripour, Slusarek, or Gadzhi publishes a live "Tag N" with real numbers. The
   `getBuildDay()` mechanic (`src/config.ts:33`, today Tag 32) + "echte Zahlen" is the durable moat.
   Recommend the homepage lead with the build-log credibility, not with the resold 997 € offer.

4. **Decide whether "from the gold-cluster" line helps.** `index.html:22` invokes the cluster by
   name. For a cold visitor who didn't know the cluster, it *introduces* the association you're
   trying to escape. Consider whether the pivot narrative is for *insiders* (then keep it deep in
   the journal) vs. *cold traffic* (then drop it from OG/meta).

## 6. Risk-question answer (explicit)

**Is the indigo/honest position both DISTINCT and CREDIBLE?**

- **DISTINCT: Yes.** The light-off-white + electric-indigo + Cormorant editorial look, plus the live
  Build-in-Public "Tag N" counter, are genuinely unoccupied versus the five gold/black/loud DACH
  players. On a thumbnail grid it does not look like the cluster.
- **CREDIBLE: Partially — and at risk.** Credibility is undercut from inside: the flagship paid
  offer IS a gold-cluster Digistore affiliate product (Mark Janzen, 997 €,
  `pro/index.html:12,204`), and the anti-hype copy climaxes on "Imperium." (`:373` /
  `index.html:963`). So the brand currently *says* honest-and-different while the *offer + climax*
  still *behave* gold-cluster. It does **not** fully vanish into the cluster — the visual + build-log
  distinction is too strong for that — but the position is **fragile**: it survives on presentation
  and would be punctured the moment a skeptic reaches the Pro funnel. Fixes §5.1–5.3 close most of
  the gap cheaply.
